Max’s Arcade

Max’s Arcade is a collection of 32 pixel-art mini games with medals, tickets and a prize counter. It also includes two full games, Halftone Hero and Embers of Ashvale. Every character and name in the app is original.

Support

Questions or problems: Comicbookhistorians@gmail.com

How to play

Tap PLAY on any game card. A short hint under each game explains its controls. The WIDE button turns a game sideways for a bigger picture. EXIT returns to the arcade.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data and never connects to the internet. Progress is saved on the device only. The full policy is on the privacy page.
