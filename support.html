Max’s Arcade

Max’s Arcade is a collection of 32 pixel-art mini games with medals, tickets, a prize counter with 24 prizes and 10 color themes, and Game Center leaderboards. It also includes two full games, Halftone Hero and Embers of Ashvale. Every character and name in the app is original.

Support

Questions or problems: Comicbookhistorians@gmail.com

How to play

Tap PLAY on any game card. A short hint under each game explains its controls. The WIDE button turns a game sideways for a bigger picture. EXIT returns to the arcade.

Leaderboards and vibration

When the device is signed in to Game Center, tap LEADERBOARDS to compare tickets, medals and Gauntlet scores with other players. Vibration can be turned off from the stats screen (tap the scoreboard at the top) under SETTINGS. HOW TO PLAY there shows the opening guide again.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. It goes online only to send Game Center scores, and only when the device is signed in to Game Center. Progress is saved on the device only. The full policy is on the privacy page.
