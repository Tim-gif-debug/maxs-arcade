Privacy Policy – Max’s Arcade

Max’s Arcade is published by Comic Book Historians LLC.

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code is included.
  • Every game, sound and font is inside the app, and the games work offline.
  • Game Center is optional. If the player is signed in to Game Center on the device, the app sends three leaderboard scores (tickets earned, medals won and best Gauntlet total) to Apple’s Game Center. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy. The developer receives no personal data from it.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Tickets and prizes are earned by playing and have no cash value.
  • Scores, tickets, settings and saved progress are stored only on the device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other device sensor.
